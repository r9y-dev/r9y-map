# Established Company Strategy

**Company Strategy Definition:**

A company strategy is a **documented** (!) master plan that guides how a business achieves its goals, beats competitors, and creates long-term value over a certain clearly described period of time.
Company strategy serves as a starting point for generating lower level strategies like Infrastructure strategy or Business development strategy.

**Company Strategy Common Practices:**

The Three Core Elements 

Diagnosis: Identifies and names the central challenge. 
A good diagnosis cuts through complex situations to find the critical bottleneck or problem blocking progress.

Guiding Policy: Outlines the overall approach chosen to overcome the obstacles found in the diagnosis. 
It acts like a guardrail, directing and constraining choices while ruling out alternative paths.

Coherent Actions: A coordinated set of practical steps, resource commitments, and behaviors designed to carry out the guiding policy. 
These actions reinforce each other rather than existing as a random list of goals.


**Benefits of clearly defined Company Strategy:**
- Better Focus: Limits distractions by defining exact boundaries of what the company will and will not do.
- Clear Alignment: Connects the big-picture goals directly to daily work so everyone moves in the same direction.
- Easier Execution: Turns abstract ideas into practical, coordinated steps that reinforce each other.
- Simple Tracking: Allows leaders to measure true progress using clear and specific metrics.


## What's next?

Here are the common steps that come after establishing company strategy:

**1. Define Clear Service Level Agreements (SLA) and Service Level Objectives (SLO):**

- Based on revenue expectations you can analyse service structure and clearly define what "good" is (when it comes to measuring reliability) 

**2. Define lower level strategies:**

- Based on expected company-wide plans and expectations, all departments can define their own plans to support this efforts and move forward in organized way

**3. Goals -> Objectives (OKRs):**

- Use lower level strategies to move from Goals to Objectives
